# C-130 inspiration

The approved replacement keeps the high wing, four turboprops, dark gunmetal charcoal, readable slate-gray military markings and hand-brushed crimson shark face. Show the four propellers with soft, translucent motion blur in still renders. The prominent M102 howitzer barrel projects from the lower starboard fuselage just aft of the wing.

- [Front three-quarter](front-three-quarter.png) shows the aircraft from below, the wrapped nose art, all four propeller sweeps and the howitzer projecting from the fuselage.
- [Starboard side](starboard-side.png) shows the aircraft's right side and the barrel end-on beneath the military stencil.
- [Overhead](overhead.png) shows the wing stencil, engine spacing and howitzer projecting to the right.

The flying model follows the earlier template's dimensions of about 58 studs across the wings, 57.6 studs nose to tail and 18.5 studs tall. The PNGs guide the shape and paint of the original geometry. [Editable aircraft and scale](../../../assets/blender/c130/README.md) · [P28](../../../plans/28-c130-model.md)
