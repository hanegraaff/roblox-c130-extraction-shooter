# Server-owned model templates

Rojo maps this folder to `ServerStorage.Assets`. [`CoverPanel.model.json`](CoverPanel.model.json) is an original anchored, collidable 16×8×2-stud primitive panel, centered at `(0,4,0)`. It reserves a template for later cover replacement; no current service clones or damages it. Its pose is authored with a twelve-component canonical `CFrame`; use the [asset authoring rules](../README.md) rather than `Position`/`Orientation` aliases. Its provenance is recorded in the [asset manifest](../manifest.json), with no external asset or script dependency.

[`C130.model.json`](C130.model.json) is an original 16-part primitive aircraft, with provenance in the manifest and no imported scripts or cloud dependency. `AircraftService` sets `Hitbox` as PrimaryPart and clones the template into Workspace. Local **−Z** is forward, **+Y** is up. The 12×10×46-stud transparent `Hitbox`, centered at local origin, is the canonical model pivot. The P06 implementation makes the 15 visible fuselage, wing, tail and engine parts queryable as well; all 16 parts are anchored, noncolliding and nontouching. The P06 plan records source-specific aircraft-damage, operated and regression evidence; the P07 plan records current candidate verification and remaining acceptance gaps.

`Hitbox.GAU23Muzzle` is at local `(−5, −2, −7)` studs; `Hitbox.M102Muzzle` is at `(−5, −2, 6)`. Their canonical CFrames are versioned and identity-oriented. They supply server-derived gunship firing origins. Model pose follows the orbit tangent, independently of the inward-facing operator camera. No separate cosmetic interpolation shell exists. The artifact gate compares all 16 part poses/sizes/flags, while real Studio specs verify canonical pivot/attachment/query/orbit behavior.

[`PanelResidual.model.json`](PanelResidual.model.json) is an original two-Part server-only template with no external asset/scripts. Left/Right are anchored, collidable/queryable, nontouching normalized strips: X width 0.25, Y/Z size 1 and X centers −0.375/+0.375. PanelGeometry reads their X width/offset and scales pristine panel clones along the longest face axis. The result preserves the original pivot/thickness and leaves a central 50% opening on walls or roofs. The current material/color comes from the original panel and its progress cue, not the normalized template. Destroyed panels retain their identity Model without BaseParts. All registered building panels share this contract, including the revised candidate’s 76 starting-shelter pieces and 111 formal-building panels; static floors/stairs/frames and generic cover are outside it.

P06 supplies a server-owned primitive held M-7; P07 generates capped physics-fragment primitives at runtime. Neither uses imported art or a cloud asset. Fragments are transient presentation and must never be exported into authored templates. Player launcher models/handling remain planned. The [manifest](../manifest.json) records source/author/license and local paths; no Roblox asset IDs or upload owner have been invented. See the [destruction owner](../../src/server/README.md#progressive-panel-destruction) for state, collision, timing and cleanup contracts.

The revised candidate generates original primitive crack/scar marks in `PanelGeometry`, with no imported texture, mesh or script dependency. Light/Heavy use Concrete/Slate and D54’s slate/charcoal/pale-fracture defaults instead of glowing CrackedLava. Marks are cosmetic on both faces, clipped to residual solids, excluded from collision/queries and structural identity, and cleaned up with owned panel state. They are runtime presentation, not separate authored templates. The fresh solo engine baseline covers appearance/query assertions. Current Warehouse fixed-scene native-input captures distinguish all normal-view tiers and the thermal Heavy hole/residual, with QA markers; shelter appearance, visual fragment falling and complete operated acceptance remain pending.
