# P18 — Game rebalancing

**Milestone:** Gameplay revision. **Dependencies:** P07, P08, P14, P17 and the integrated Prism Seedship (PR #62). **Requirements:** R03, R05–R10, R12, R13, R15–R18, R22. Status: [roadmap](../roadmap.md).

## Capability and player experience

Scale aircraft durability to the Ground team, make building panels quick to demolish, remove the circular camera boundary that can trap directional controls, place a fourth recoverable rocket launcher inside the alien spaceship, and show Ground players in thermal red against a darker environment.

The 2026-11-23 user revision explicitly selects **1,000 health per Ground player**, counted from the deployment roster, and **existing fixed rocket damage** (100 health units). The operator is excluded. Departures, deaths, spectators and late arrivals cannot resize or refill the current aircraft. The former three-rocket guarantee is superseded. M-7 damage remains 5 units.

One direct 105mm M102 impact destroys one registered wall/roof panel. Two earned seconds of valid same-panel 30mm impacts destroy a pristine panel. Interrupted firing retains earned damage but never counts paused time. Persistent Light/Heavy visuals remain useful during the shortened 30mm progression; howitzer fire may go straight to Total. Floors, stairs and supporting frames remain stable. The waiting lobby remains protected. Apply the shared rule to every battlefield building, including Sandbreak and the Seedship.

Operator WASD and touch arrows move smoothly across the map's world-aligned **720 × 720-stud square**, centered on the existing level center. Only the square edges limit pan position. At an edge, an unblocked axis must continue; reverse input must recover immediately at every orbit phase and both zooms. The former disk/frustum pan clipping is superseded. Keep the canonical aircraft orbit and independent mouse/touch aiming. Keep camera math finite, directions consistent, selection/recenter and role cleanup usable, including near-overhead views. Server combat still validates its own playable aim envelope and solid cover; extending presentation bounds grants no damage authority.

Retain the three existing launcher sites and add a stable, reachable fourth site inside the Prism Seedship. Each Ground player still carries at most one. Reuse atomic pickup, reload-preserving transfer/recovery, direct rocket damage and reset ownership.

The user added item 5 during implementation: Ground players in the operator view should be **thermal red**, and the overly bright ground must be muted for clear contrast. The user subsequently allowed keeping the whole view desaturated if that is easier; darker terrain and clear target contrast take priority. Preserve building silhouettes, major attached details, actual openings and solid-cover occlusion. Keep the Ground role's normal materials intact. Colors/brightness are presentation tuning defaults; the selected red or grayscale appearance and improved target contrast require rendered review at both weapon zooms.

Invalid counts/rounds reject without mutation. Two actual Studio clients observe identical maximum/current health and a fresh-round reset.

Native Studio rays and two-client observations prove actual openings on representative wall/roof geometry, including Seedship and Sandbreak coverage.
- **A3 — Square camera movement:** Deterministic tests cover all square edges/corners, axis sliding, immediate reversal, normalized diagonal movement, invalid input, frame-time cap, both profiles and sampled full orbit. Real Studio camera/input tests verify smooth WASD and touch-pad directions, independent aim, both zooms/recenter, finite near-overhead frames, no circular-boundary jam, and cleanup without changing the aircraft orbit. The operator's own airframe must not obscure targeting; Ground still sees and can hit it. Record focused operated review separately from numeric assertions.
- **A4 — Seedship launcher:** Git reconstruction and native supported/clearance/navigation checks prove four unique reachable anchors and the fourth is inside the ship. Two actual clients exercise raced claims, one carrier limit, independent identities, fixed rocket damage, reload-preserving drop/recovery and reset to all four anchors. - **A5 — Delivery:** The final full `python3 scripts/verify.py` gate passes nonempty required suites, source-bound Studio smoke, the current **three-client P18** scenario and adapted **two-client P05** actual fire/flight/opening regression pass, independent tester findings are resolved, and player/contributor/design documentation describes the revised whole experience. Keep operated input/navigation/rendered checks separate from numeric fixtures. Historical incompatible selectors reject explicitly and supply no passing/skipped gameplay evidence. Source/evidence are committed and pushed with a reviewable PR. P12's separate live release/capacity gates are not prerequisites for this explicitly requested local revision and are not waived or completed by it.
- **A6 — Thermal readability:** Native client tests verify clearly contrasting living-Ground highlights, muted operator-only scenery/terrain/backdrop, opaque cover and exact restoration on role loss/teardown. Assert actual visible surfaces, rather than the intentionally invisible ground proxy. Rendered review at both FOVs shows exposed Ground targets clearly against darker terrain, covered players hidden and a player visible through a real earned opening. Red is the initial candidate; the user also accepts a fully desaturated view. Record the actual selected presentation, camera/render settings and observations separately from property assertions.
