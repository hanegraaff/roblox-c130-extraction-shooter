# P34 — Prepare the C-130 Assault Roblox page

**Depends on** P27–P31. **Requirements** R31. [Roadmap](../roadmap.md#mvp-completion) · [Requirements](../requirements.md)

A player scanning Roblox on a phone can see the two roles and understand the hook in seconds.

## Work

- Use the exact title **C-130 Assault**. Write the short hook and description in an energetic arcade voice. A starting line to test is “One gunship. One ground crew. Bring the plane down before it finds you.” Match the final text to the tested experience and controls.
- Capture actual gameplay from the candidate showing the plane from Ground, the thermal view over destructible cover, and a launcher or medkit moment. Create an original square icon, **AI-generated wide banner images**, and thumbnail variants. Use the game’s aircraft, environment and colors as visual references so the artwork is recognizable beside the screenshots.
- Lead with an image that makes the matchup clear at a glance. Use the next images to show each role’s view. Coordinate the palette and typography across the icon and banners and keep the subjects readable in small crops. Keep editable artwork, final exports, banner generation provenance, gameplay capture revisions and listing copy together.
- Check Creator Dashboard’s current upload slots, dimensions and crop previews at production time. Prepare a polished desktop and phone listing preview for the owner. P35 owns the live listing change.

## Acceptance

1. **A1 · Content review.** Read the hook and description beside the real screenshots under the exact title **C-130 Assault**. A new reader can name both roles and their objectives. Confirm the copy describes available features and supported devices.
2. **A2 · Visual review.** Preview the complete page with its icon, AI-generated banners, gameplay screenshots and thumbnails at phone and desktop sizes. Check that the artwork and copy read together, the focal subjects survive the actual listing crops, and each export loads from the delivery package. Record the selected art and copy.

Run the applicable [project checks](../docs/testing.md) and record the source revision, environment and observed results for each criterion.

## Documentation

Give the owner a short file-to-listing-slot guide. Identify which images are AI-generated promotional art and which are captures of the tested game.
