# P34 — Fast-paced arcade game page

**Depends on** P27–P32. **Requirements** R31. [Roadmap](../roadmap.md#public-mvp-launch) · [Requirements](../requirements.md)

A player scanning Roblox on a phone can see the two roles and understand the hook in seconds.

## Work

- Write the title, short hook and game description in an energetic arcade voice. A starting line to test is “One gunship. One ground crew. Bring the plane down before it finds you.” Match the final text to the tested experience and controls.
- Capture actual gameplay from the launch candidate: the plane from Ground, the thermal view over destructible cover, and a launcher or medkit moment. Create an original square icon, wide banner/key art and thumbnail variants. Keep recognizable subjects clear near the center for small screens and crops.
- Keep editable artwork, final exports, capture provenance and listing copy together. Check the current Creator Dashboard upload slots, dimensions and crop previews at production time. Prepare a preview for review. P35 owns the live listing change.

## Acceptance

1. **A1 · Content review.** Read the hook and description beside the real screenshots. A new reader can name both roles and their objectives. Confirm the copy describes available features and supported devices.
2. **A2 · Visual review.** Preview the icon and thumbnails at phone and desktop sizes. Verify the assets fit the actual listing slots, read clearly after cropping and load from the delivery package. Record the selected art and copy.

Run the applicable [project checks](../docs/testing.md) and record the source revision, environment and observed results for each criterion.

## Documentation

Give the owner a short file-to-listing-slot guide and keep promotional art in the listing package.
